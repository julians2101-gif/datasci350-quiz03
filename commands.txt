Task 2 - Create the commentator Modelfile

File:
ollama/Modelfile

Explanation:
FROM selects granite4:350m as the base model.
temperature 0.7 controls randomness in responses.
top_p 0.9 limits token sampling.
SYSTEM establishes an excited sports commentator persona.

The three rules require answers to have at most four
sentences, address the user as "sports fans", and admit
uncertainty on air.
